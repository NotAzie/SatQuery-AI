Agent

A specialized autonomous component responsible for one scientific task within an investigation.

---

Connector

A module that acquires data from an external source.

---

Earth Object

A versioned, provider-neutral semantic entity explicitly materialized from
canonical observations and evidence. Its assertions retain source links,
provenance, uncertainty, temporal validity, and reproducibility metadata.
Unknown properties remain unset; materialization does not itself detect an
entity or establish a scientific conclusion.

---

Earth Memory

Persistent knowledge accumulated from investigations.

---

Evidence

Information supporting or contradicting a hypothesis.

---

Hypothesis

A possible explanation evaluated during reasoning.

---

Investigation

A reproducible workflow executed to answer a scientific question.

---

Mission

The high-level objective created from a user request.

---

Observation

Raw information collected from one or more datasets.

---

Reasoning

The process of combining evidence into conclusions.

---

Verification

Attempting to disprove conclusions before accepting them.
